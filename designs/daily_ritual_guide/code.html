Deepa Prajwalana

Light the lamp (diya) facing east or north. As the flame rises, let it symbolize the dispelling of darkness and ignorance from your mind.

शुभं करोति कल्याणमारोग्यं धनसंपदा ।
शत्रुबुद्धिविनाशाय दीपज्योतिर्नमोऽस्तु ते ॥

Shubham Karoti Kalyaanam-Aarogyam Dhana-Sampadaa |
Shatru-Buddhi-Vinaashaaya Deepa-Jyotir-Namo-Astu Te ||
